## The Problem

Travel operations are fragmented. A typical OTA juggles 5+ disconnected tools for reservations, invoicing, visa processing, supplier management, and client communications. Each handoff is a potential failure point.

## The Solution: Multi-Agent Orchestration

Instead of replacing tools one by one, we built an autonomous agent fleet that orchestrates the entire workflow:

- **Reservation Agent** — handles booking requests, checks availability, confirms with suppliers
- **Invoice Agent** — generates invoices, tracks payments, sends reminders
- **Visa Agent** — processes visa applications, checks requirements, follows up
- **Supplier Agent** — manages hotel/airline relationships, negotiates rates
- **Client Agent** — handles inquiries, provides updates, escalates when needed

## Results

- **90% automation** of manual workflows
- **80% self-service** for client inquiries
- **SAR 16M+** annual revenue managed
- **300+ corporate accounts** served

## What's Next

The next evolution is predictive — agents that don't just execute but anticipate. Pricing that adjusts before demand shifts. Support that reaches out before clients notice issues. Systems that learn from every interaction.

This is where travel technology is heading. Not just digital, but autonomous.
