## Beyond Chatbots

A concierge isn't a chatbot. It remembers preferences, anticipates needs, and operates across languages and cultural contexts. Building one requires more than an LLM wrapper.

## Architecture

Our AI concierge stack:

1. **RAG Layer** — retrieves guest history, preferences, and contextual data
2. **Multi-Agent Router** — delegates to specialized agents (booking, dining, experiences)
3. **Memory System** — tracks conversation context and guest preferences across sessions
4. **Multilingual Pipeline** — Arabic, English, with cultural context awareness
5. **Human Escalation** — seamless handoff to human staff for complex requests

## Key Decisions

- **Vector DB for memory** — semantic search over guest history beats keyword matching
- **Agent specialization** — one agent per domain beats a single generalist
- **Confidence routing** — low-confidence responses trigger human review automatically

## Impact

- 98% client satisfaction score
- 80%+ autonomous resolution
- Response time: < 2 seconds for 90% of queries
